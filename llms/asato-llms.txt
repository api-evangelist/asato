# Asato
> Asato is an agentic AI platform that helps enterprise IT leaders — CIOs in
> healthcare, financial services, and public sector — discover, curate, and
> optimize their complete IT asset landscape: SaaS licenses, hardware, and
> compliance. AI agents continuously reconcile fragmented data from tools like
> CASB, IDP, and ERP into a single trusted, decision-grade inventory, then
> autonomously execute optimization actions (reclaiming unused licenses,
> flagging aging hardware, closing compliance gaps) with human-in-the-loop
> control. Backed by Intel Capital. Channel partner: CDW Corporation.

## Blogs

- [Your IT Data Isn't Dirty, It's Multi-Truth](https://www.asato.ai/blog/your-it-data-isnt-dirty-its-multi-truth): Part 1 of the "Closing the IT Data Trust Gap" series. Explains why enterprise IT numbers contradict each other — each source system is locally correct but there's no unified entity model. Defines the four trust killers: identity inflation, application inflation, noisy Shadow IT, and fragmented spend.
- [Why Your User Count Is 3X Your Headcount](https://www.asato.ai/blog/why-your-user-count-is-3x-your-headcount-and-why-thats-not-a-bug): Part 2. How identity providers inflate user counts with service accounts, guests, and disabled accounts — and how Asato's identity funnel fixes this for accurate licensing and vendor negotiations.
- [You Don't Have 6,000 Apps. You Have 60](https://www.asato.ai/blog/you-dont-have-6-000-apps-you-have-60): Part 3. How CASB tools inflate app inventories with CDNs and infrastructure noise, and how Asato's application funnel surfaces only the tools employees actually use.
- [What "Decision-Grade" Data Actually Looks Like](https://www.asato.ai/blog/what-decision-grade-data-actually-looks-like): Part 6 (series conclusion). Defines the five properties of decision-grade data — normalized, classified, linked, scoped, scored — and the measurable outcomes they unlock.
- [Business Observability – A New Frontier in Enterprise Asset Visibility](https://www.asato.ai/blog/business-observability-a-new-frontier-in-enterprise-asset-visibility): Founders introduce "Business Observability" — a metadata-driven intelligence layer that turns CIO questions into contextual, actionable answers via agents and an Asset Knowledge Graph.
- [How We're Building Business Observability at Asato](https://www.asato.ai/blog/how-were-building-business-observability-at-asato): CTO Vinay Saxena on the AI-native technical architecture: Kubernetes-native, LangGraph + MCP/A2A agent orchestration, semantic ingestion, and the Asset Knowledge Graph.
